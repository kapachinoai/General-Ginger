    Questions

    Can I buy $GNG now?

    No. $GNG has not been created, so anyone selling it now is a scammer. We will never message you asking for money or crypto.

    Do the games cost anything?

    No. The games are free to play, and you never pay to earn points.

    Are points worth money?

    No. Points have no cash value. If $GNG launches, verified points decide your share of the airdrop pool, but the token's value is not promised and could be zero.

    When is the airdrop?

    There is no date yet. Rules for digital assets are different in every country (in Nigeria, for example, they are regulated as securities), so we will only launch after a legal review, and only where the law allows. Joining the waitlist is the best way to hear first.

    Who can join?

    Anyone 18 or older, anywhere in the world, can play and join the waitlist. Whether you can receive $GNG depends on the law where you live, so some countries may be left out of the airdrop. We can't accept sign-ups from countries under full international sanctions.

    Can I play from my country?

    Yes. The game is free and works in any country with a web browser. Phone numbers need your country code, like +44 or +1. Daily point limits reset at midnight UTC.

    What do you do with my details?

    We only use your email or phone number to contact you about General Ginger and the launch. We don't sell it, and you can ask us for a copy or to delete it at any time, wherever you live.
